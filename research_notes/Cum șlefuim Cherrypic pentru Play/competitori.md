# Competitive landscape on Google Play (2025–2026): gallery cleaners / swipe sorters and AI photo organizers and editors, for positioning Cherrypic

Method note: this session's network blocked direct fetches of play.google.com, appbrain.com, swipephotos.com and swipephotocleaner.com (DNS refused). Every figure below comes from search-result snippets of third-party trackers (AppGoblin, AppPricingLab, AppFollow, MWM/Spark, Similarweb), vendor pages and tech press. Tracker numbers often disagree, so treat ratings and installs as approximate. Check them on live Play listings (gl=RO) before publishing. I found almost no EU or RON prices. Most prices are USD, from iOS listings or vendor blogs.

## Q1. Competitor features, on-device vs cloud, pricing, rating, downloads, update recency

### Takeaway
The swipe-cleaner niche on Android is crowded but shallow. Slidebox and MWM's Swipewipe are the only established names (4.4–4.6★, roughly 0.5–1M+ Android installs). Strong iOS names (Clever Cleaner) are not on Android at all. AI organizing and editing is dominated by platform giants (Google Photos, Samsung Gallery) and cloud subscription editors (Remini, Picsart, Photoroom, Lightroom Premium). Many of their AI features are cloud-based, gated by region or device, or paywalled.

### Inferences
- **Pricing benchmark** for the cleaner niche is weekly/yearly subscriptions ($9.99/week, $29.99/year Swipewipe; about $5/month Slidebox). For editors it is $4.99–12.99/month (Lightroom Premium, Picsart Gold) and up to about $80/year or more (Remini). A free, no-server Cherrypic undercuts all of them on price.
- **Download tiers.** Android swipe leaders are in the 500K–1M+ install tier. Editors are in the 100M–1B+ tier. Google Photos and Samsung Gallery are preinstalled. Realistically, Cherrypic competes for attention with Slidebox and Swipewipe, not with Picsart.
- **On-device versus cloud.** Google Photos' grouping (Stacks) and generative editing lean on the cloud: Stacks need backup, and edits run locally or in the cloud by size. Remini, Photoroom and Lightroom Generative Remove run on servers. "Nothing leaves your phone, no account" is a real differentiator, especially in the EU.

## Q2. What makes the top ones popular (hook, onboarding, viral loops)

### Takeaway
The swipe apps grew on a single tactile hook ("Tinder for your camera roll") amplified by TikTok creator campaigns. The editors spike on viral AI-trend formats (cartoon or avatar styles) bought through ads. Neither group has a retention ritual built on personal taste.

### Cited Findings
- MWM's Swipewipe strategy relied on TikTok creator collaborations that "went viral and drew attention from organic creators." MWM acquired Swipewipe after it reached about 5M users in under a year (MWM's own claim) — [MWM journal](https://www.mwm.ai/journal/swipewipe-rising-on-the-app-charts-in-just-90-days); [Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/mwm-acquires-swipewipe-for-mobile-app-leadership)
- A case study describes an iPhone swipe app beating a crowded niche through creative TikTok ads — [undefinedapps via HN reader](https://hn.jakechampion.name/reader/https://www.undefinedapps.com/post/iphone-app-beat-crowded-niche-creativity-tiktok-ads)
- Press frames the category as cleaning up storage "in pure Tinder style" — [Xataka Móvil](https://www.xatakamovil.com/aplicaciones/esta-app-promete-acabar-problemas-almacenamiento-tu-movil-al-puro-estilo-tinder)
- Month-by-month organization (Swipewipe, Swipli) gives users bite-sized, completable sessions — [MWM/Spark](https://mwm.ai/mwm-studio/apps/swipewipe); [meerabel](https://www.meerabel.com/en/blog/best-photo-deleting-apps/)
- Remini's 2025 spike came from the AI-cartoon trend: downloads doubled, but Appfigures judged it ad-driven, with flat revenue — [Appfigures](https://www.appfigures.com/resources/insights/20250404?f=4)
- Clever Cleaner's hook is being "completely free, no ads" in a niche full of weekly paywalls — [thebusinessdive](https://thebusinessdive.com/?p=11442)

### Inferences
- Viral hooks that work: (1) a satisfying swipe gesture that films well, (2) a "GB freed" or "photos deleted" counter (implied by the storage-cleanup framing), (3) "free, no ads" in a niche known for aggressive weekly subscriptions.
- A Cherrypic loop could be a shareable "today's pick" or "best of the month" card. That combines the swipe hook with a reason to come back daily.

### Gaps
- No public data on onboarding flows, paywall placement or retention for Slidebox or Swipewipe, and no app-intelligence numbers (Sensor Tower/AppMagic were not reachable).

## Q3. Who learns personal taste, picks the "best shot", or runs a daily sorting ritual

### Takeaway
On consumer Android, best-shot selection exists only as a generic, non-personalized feature (Google Photos Stacks "top pick", Ask Photos curation). Taste learning exists only in desktop/pro cullers (Aftershoot, Imagen). No mobile swipe cleaner found learns from the user's keep/delete choices or frames sorting as a daily ritual.

### Cited Findings
- Google Photos Stacks automatically choose the "top pick that best captures that moment," and the user can override it. This is generic AI with no stated learning from the user's choices — [Google Photos Help](https://support.google.com/photos/answer/14169846)
- Ask Photos can "curate a selection of the best pictures" — [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/google-photos-ask-photos-3443283)
- Aftershoot says it learns "your preferences, patterns and creative instincts." Its pricing starts around $15/month for culling, desktop only — [Aftershoot](https://aftershoot.com/selects/); [Guideflow](https://www.guideflow.com/blog/photo-culling-software)
- Imagen offers a Personal AI Profile trained on past Lightroom catalogs (pro, desktop) — [Imagen](https://imagen-ai.com/valuable-tips/best-ai-photo-culling-software-2026/)
- Narrative Select is described as lightweight with on-device AI that syncs with Lightroom Mobile collections. It assists manual choices, and no taste learning was confirmed — [Guideflow](https://www.guideflow.com/blog/photo-culling-software)
- Swipe cleaners organize by month (Swipewipe, Swipli). None of the sources describe daily prompts or preference learning — [meerabel](https://www.meerabel.com/en/blog/best-photo-deleting-apps/); [MWM/Spark](https://mwm.ai/mwm-studio/apps/swipewipe)
- One tracker notes Swipli does not clearly advertise automatic duplicate clustering. Several swipe apps are only a swipe interface with no AI grouping — [meerabel](https://www.meerabel.com/en/blog/best-photo-deleting-apps/)

### Inferences
- "A personal culler that learns your taste" is unclaimed on Google Play. It is proven valuable in the pro market (Aftershoot and Imagen charge about $15/month or more) but has not been brought to consumer phones.
- A "daily keep / not today" ritual is different from the cleaners' month-by-month backlog model. It is a habit loop, not a one-off cleanup.

### Gaps
- Pixel camera Best Take / Auto Best Take (merging faces across bursts) was not researched with sources. It is a camera feature, not gallery culling.
- I could not verify whether any small 2026 Android newcomer claims taste learning. Searches surfaced none.

## Q4. Where is the clear gap Cherrypic could own on Android?

### Takeaway
The open spot is a free, ad-free, fully on-device app that does all four of: (1) group duplicates, bursts and blurry shots and pick the best, (2) learn the user's own taste from swipes, (3) build a daily ritual, and (4) add a light on-device editor. Today users have to stitch together a cleaner (Slidebox/Swipewipe, subscription), Google Photos (cloud, generic picks) and an editor (Remini/Picsart/Lightroom, cloud and paywalls).

### Cited Findings
- The free leader, Clever Cleaner, is iOS-only. Android has no equivalent strong free cleaner, and Android swipe leaders charge $5/month to $9.99/week — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1666645584); [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/slidebox/about); [Wipix](https://wipix.app/blog/best-photo-cleaner-apps-android-2026)
- Google Photos' best-shot grouping needs cloud backup — [Google Photos Help](https://support.google.com/photos/answer/14169846)
- Google's newest AI editing was rolled out in the US first, and processing can go to the cloud — [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/apps-software/google-photos-conversational-editing-rolling-out-to-more-users); [BGR](https://bgr.com/1976304/google-photos-conversational-editing-android/)
- Samsung's best AI Gallery features are tied to new Galaxy flagships, with an unconfirmed rollout to older devices and quality complaints (cartoonish results, forced smoothing) — [letsdatascience](https://letsdatascience.com/news/samsung-adds-generative-ai-editing-to-gallery-89bbc52f); [webpronews](https://www.webpronews.com/samsung-puts-advanced-ai-photo-editing-directly-into-gallery-app/)
- Editors paywall object removal and enhancement: Lightroom Generative Remove is Premium-only, and Remini costs about $80/year or more — [Adobe Help](https://helpx.adobe.com/ro/lightroom/mobile/app-subscriptions-and-account/upgrade-to-paid-subscription.html); [techeconomy.ng](https://techeconomy.ng/four-things-to-know-about-remini-ai-photo-editing-app)
- Snapseed on Android went years without a major update, and 3.0 was still pending as of Jan 2026, which leaves room for a modern free editor — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/01/06/snapseed-android-redesign/)
- Fully offline AI editors that do exist are mostly iOS-only; Android examples are niche single-function apps (RendrFlow for upscaling, AI Retouch for removal) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6758233962); [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.ai.retouch.remove)

### Inferences
- Positioning line: "The free culler that learns what *you* like, entirely on your phone." It has three proof points no single Android rival combines: taste learning, an on-device best-of-burst pick plus a daily ritual, and free on-device editing (eraser, clarity upscale, white-background cutout) where Photoroom, Remini and Lightroom charge.
- The cost to Cherrypic of being free is near zero because it has no server inference. For subscription rivals, matching "free" would destroy their business model, so the price difference is defensible.
- Cherrypic should avoid competing head-on on generative features ("Help me edit", Photo Assist prompts). Better to stress privacy, offline use, works on any Android, and no account. That message is attractive in the EU/Romania, where Google and Samsung generative features arrive later or region-limited (inference from US-first rollouts).
- The biggest threat is Google adding taste-aware picks to Photos, or MWM adding AI best-pick to Swipewipe. Speed and the "learns you" story are the moat.

### Gaps
- No verified data on availability in Romania for Google/Samsung AI features, and no RON prices for any competitor.
- No user-review mining (Play reviews were not accessible) to quantify complaints such as paywall frustration or deleting the wrong photo.
